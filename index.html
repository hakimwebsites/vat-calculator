UAE VAT Basics for Businesses

  • Standard VAT rate in the UAE is 5%, applied to most goods and services
  • Businesses with taxable supplies over AED 375,000/year must register for VAT with the Federal Tax Authority (FTA)
  • Voluntary registration is available from AED 187,500/year in taxable supplies
  • VAT returns are typically filed quarterly, though some businesses file monthly

This tool provides estimates for quick reference only and is not tax advice. Always confirm VAT treatment and filing obligations with the FTA or a registered tax agent.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to register for VAT?

Registration is mandatory once your taxable supplies exceed AED 375,000 in the past 12 months, or are expected to in the next 30 days. Below that, registration is optional starting from AED 187,500.

What does "zero-rated" mean?

Zero-rated supplies (like certain exports, international transport, and some healthcare/education services) are taxable but charged at 0% VAT, which is different from being fully exempt.

Can I use this for official invoices?

This tool helps you calculate the correct VAT breakdown for quotes and internal reference. For fully compliant tax invoices, ensure you include your TRN (Tax Registration Number) and all FTA-required invoice fields.
